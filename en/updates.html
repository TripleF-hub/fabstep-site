What changed.

Every FabStep version, with what was added or improved. Updates install from the App Store.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Version 1.1 · coming soon

This version is being prepared. It will be available as soon as Apple approves it.

  • FabStep now speaks French and English. The app on iPhone, on Apple Watch and in widgets follows your device's language. So do dates, numbers, units and permission prompts (Health, location, motion).
  • “Start a walk” right under your steps. The card now sits directly below the step counter, so you can start a walk without scrolling.

Version 1.0 · October 2026

First public release.

  • Counts your steps on iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Shows your live heart rate from the watch.
  • Records your walks with the GPS route, pace and live steps.
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and the watch face.
  • No account, no ads, no subscription: no data leaves your device.
